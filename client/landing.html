How it works

  1. 1

    Create a poll

    Sign up, name your poll, and get a shareable join link and QR code in seconds.

  2. 2

    People join

    Participants scan the QR or open the link and add their community. No login, no friction.

  3. 3

    Watch it live

    The world map fills with flags in real time. Put it on the big screen and let the room watch it grow.
